## Critical incident

- **When:** after several months of negative returns.
- **Trigger (what they saw first):** daily returns on the MF dashboard below the amount invested.
- **What they looked at (in app / outside):** the MF dashboard every day; financial YouTubers.
- **Who they asked, what they were told:** YouTubers, whose recommendations had earlier led them to change funds.
- **Options they knew of:** continue or stop; didn't mention reduce or skip.
- **What they did, and how it ended:** stopped the SIP and left the existing investment as it is; new savings go to FD / RD.
- **What was missing:** "more insights on the funds" they hold; returns that matched the expectation.
