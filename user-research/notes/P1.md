## Critical incident

- **When:** around month 12, when the SIP was "bleeding red".
- **Trigger (what they saw first):** red returns on the dashboard after a full year of instalments.
- **What they looked at (in app / outside):** invested amount and profit/loss in the app; articles online.
- **Who they asked, what they were told:** the colleague who suggested the SIP: "just hold on and be patient". An article said a red SIP is a good time to invest.
- **Options they knew of:** continue, or stop and move the money to an RD.
- **What they did, and how it ended:** kept the SIP running, partly on the colleague's word, partly because "I failed [to understand] since I am new to SIP".
- **What was missing:** a way to tell whether a red SIP at month 12 is normal; what might happen next.

## What they check

- **How often, what prompts it:** daily in the first months, now twice a week.
- **How they explain the number they look at (their words):** only "my invested amount and the profit or loss"; didn't know XIRR and checked it with an LLM.

## Payment

- **Any failed or delayed instalment? How found out, what happened:** yes, once, for low balance. Found out only by opening the app and checking transaction history. No notification remembered. SIP continued.
