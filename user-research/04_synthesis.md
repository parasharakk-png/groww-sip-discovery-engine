## Step 2 · Affinity map (30–45 min)

Put every quote and observation on its own sticky note (FigJam or paper). Group them bottom-up, without
starting from the hypotheses, then name each group as a user need in the participant's voice
("I need to know if this is normal"). Look for:

- the **moment** continuation breaks (what was on screen, what time in the journey);
- the **root cause** behind the trigger (missing reference point? missing option? missing information?);
- **workarounds** (who they asked, what they searched, what they did instead);
- what **continuers** had that stoppers didn't.

## Step 4 · Draft the problem statement (input to Part 4)

> **For** [segment], **in** [moment in the first 12 months], **continuation breaks because** [root cause].
> **Today they** [workaround], **which** [why it fails]. **If we solve it**, [user value], and the business gains
> [product outcome → metric].

Avoid "investors stop their SIPs when markets fall": the brief rules it out. Name the *why* behind the moment.

## Step 5 · Pick quotes for the deck

Choose 3 quotes: one for the moment, one for the root cause, one for the workaround. Label them P1–P6 only.
