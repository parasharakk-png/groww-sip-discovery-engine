# Screener (Google Form)

## Recruitment message

> Hi! I'm doing a short research project on how people experience their **first SIP / mutual fund
> investment** in the first year. I'm looking for people who started their first SIP in the last 1–1.5 years —
> whether you're still investing, paused, or stopped. It's a 40-minute video chat about your experience,
> **no financial details needed** (no account numbers, no amounts, no screenshots). Nothing is being sold and
> there are no right answers. If you're up for it, this 2-minute form helps me pick a mix of people: [link]

Optional thank-you: a ₹200–300 voucher or coffee. Mention it in the message if you offer one.

## Form questions

Q1–Q7 are the qualifying questions. **Qualify** marks the answers that keep a respondent in; **Drop** marks the
answers that rule them out.

**Q1. When did you start your first-ever SIP or mutual fund investment?**
- Less than 3 months ago → Drop (too early)
- 3–6 months ago → Qualify
- 6–12 months ago → Qualify
- 12–18 months ago → Qualify
- More than 18 months ago → Drop

**Q2. Before that SIP, had you invested in mutual funds before?**
- No, it was my first → Qualify
- Yes → Drop

**Q3. Which app do you mainly use for it?**
- Groww → Qualify (preferred)
- Zerodha Coin / Kite, Paytm Money, ET Money, Kuvera, INDmoney → Qualify
- Bank / directly with the fund house / an advisor → Drop

**Q4. What is the status of that SIP today?** (pick the closest)
- Still running, I've never thought of stopping
- Still running, but I have seriously considered stopping or pausing
- I paused it or reduced the amount
- I stopped it
- I stopped it and started a different one instead
- An instalment failed / it stopped without me doing anything
- Not sure

**Q5. Your age**
- Under 22 · 22–26 · 27–32 · 33–40 · Over 40 → **Qualify: 22–32** (allow one 33–40 if needed)

**Q6. How do you mainly earn?**
- Salaried · Self-employed / freelance · Student · Other → **Qualify: salaried** (allow one non-salaried)

**Q7. Do you work in finance (banking, investing, wealth, fintech product)?**
- Yes → Drop · No → Qualify

**Q8. Roughly how much is the SIP per month?**
- Up to ₹1,000 · ₹1,001–5,000 · ₹5,001–10,000 · More than ₹10,000 (range only; used for the mix, not to drop)

**Q9. Since starting, have you changed job or the bank account your salary goes to?**
- Yes · No

**Q10. Contact (WhatsApp or email) and two time slots that suit you this week**

## Picking 6 from the replies

| Slot | Q4 answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 2–3 people | considered stopping · paused / reduced · stopped · stopped and started another | Core of H1–H4 |
| 2 people | still running, never thought of stopping | Contrast: what helped them continue |
| 1 person | instalment failed, or Q9 = Yes | Problem 1 probe (H5) |

Also aim for at least two who started 6–18 months ago, a spread across Q8 bands, and no more than two people
from the same company or college.
